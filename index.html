Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

“The Missile Man of India”

Biography

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He was born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

Kalam played an important role in India's space and missile development programmes. He worked with organizations such as ISRO and DRDO and became widely known for his contribution to India's aerospace and defence programmes.

He served as the President of India from 2002 to 2007. He was also a popular author and inspired millions of students through his speeches and books.

Major Achievements

  • Played a key role in India's space and missile programmes.
  • Served as the 11th President of India.
  • Received the Bharat Ratna in 1997.
  • Received the Padma Bhushan and Padma Vibhushan.
  • Authored books including Wings of Fire.

Legacy

Dr. Kalam is remembered for his contribution to science, education and India's technological development. His life continues to inspire students and young people to pursue knowledge, innovation and their dreams.
